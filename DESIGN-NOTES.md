# Alazal: from a point to a whole world

Direction: a long-form brand story for Iraqi students and parents. The primary action is to speak with Alazal about the student's next step. The original DIN Next LT Arabic, navy #100051, teal #06ffac, logo proportions, factual timeline are fixed requirements.

## Page outline

1. A monumental opening logo, its original dot extruded in 3D.
2. Five scroll-driven scenes: printed pages (2010), a classroom assembling (2012), two school entrances (2020 and 2024), a digital screen (2026), the identity returning together today.
3. A large word-by-word statement about the student, with three concrete commitments.
4. Alazal institutions and their direct contact routes.
5. Teacher directory omitted per the latest content direction.
6. Practical questions and a final contact invitation.

## Next steps

Choose the institution, contact its team, discuss the student's stage and the available registration details. Do not invent fees, guarantees, results, trials or schedules.

## Practical questions

- Where did Alazal begin? A library and publishing house in Kadhimiya in 2010.
- What does the group include? Library, publishing house, institute, girls' and boys' secondary schools, and digital platform.
- How do I register at the institute? The existing registration form or the institute's WhatsApp.
- Are there separate girls' and boys' schools? Yes, founded in 2020 and 2024 respectively.
- Where can I confirm fees and times? Directly with the relevant institution through the listed contact links.

## Motion contract

The starting dot uses the original logo coordinates and rotation. Scroll initiates all movement. Each scene has a distinct physical action. Compact phones and landscape screens retain all five moving scenes. Reading mode and reduced-motion preferences expose every section in normal flow. The page has no teacher directory or portrait gallery. The 3D module renders on demand and stays within its compressed asset budget.

Latest direction: reduce every story stop to a headline and one short sentence, let the images and movement carry the narrative, remove teacher profiles and portraits. The arrival guide now adds verified streets, selectable starting points, walking paths, written addresses and complete contacts.
